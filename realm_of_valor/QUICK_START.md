# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Realm of Valor Enhanced Features

## Map Features - Get Started in 5 Minutes

### 1. View Trails on Map

Trails are now automatically displayed with color-coded difficulty:

- **🟢 Green**: Easy trails (great for beginners)
- **🔵 Blue**: Moderate trails (standard difficulty)
- **🟠 Orange**: Hard trails (challenging)
- **🔴 Red**: Expert trails (very difficult)

Simply open the map and you'll see all trails in your area!

### 2. Tap a Trail to See Details

When you tap any trail marker, you'll see:

**Info Tab**:
- Full description
- Distance & elevation gain
- Estimated rewards (Gold, XP, buffs)
- Weather conditions
- Safety information
- Tags and features

**Leaderboard Tab**:
- Top 10 fastest times
- Your personal best
- Strava segment rankings
- Friend completions

**Elevation Tab**:
- Elevation profile graph
- Total gain/loss
- Grade percentages

**Social Tab**:
- Friends who completed
- Recent activities
- Community posts

### 3. Start a Trail Quest

Tap **"Start Trail"** button to:
- Create automatic quest
- Set geofences at checkpoints
- Begin location tracking
- Track your progress
- Earn rewards upon completion

### 4. Earn Rewards

Complete trails to earn:
- **Gold**: Based on distance + elevation
  - 1 gold per 0.5 km
  - 1 gold per 100m elevation
  
- **XP**: Based on difficulty
  - Easy: 1.0x multiplier
  - Moderate: 1.5x multiplier
  - Hard: 2.0x multiplier
  - Expert: 3.0x multiplier

- **Temporary Buffs** (if high HR maintained):
  - +2-5 ATK bonus
  - +1-3 DEF bonus
  - +5-15 HP bonus
  - Lasts 10 minutes

- **Cards**: Difficulty-based rewards
  - Easy → Common cards
  - Moderate → Uncommon cards
  - Hard → Rare cards
  - Expert → Epic cards + badges

### 5. Connect Strava (Optional)

For enhanced features:
1. Go to Profile → Fitness Trackers
2. Connect Strava account
3. Get automatic activity sync
4. See segment leaderboards
5. Track personal bests
6. Earn bonus rewards for PRs

## Quest Features

### Quest Types Now on Map:

1. **⚔️ Battle Quests** (Red markers)
   - Defeat enemies
   - XP + Gold rewards
   - Random loot drops

2. **💎 Treasure Quests** (Yellow markers)
   - Find items
   - Higher gold rewards
   - Rare card drops

3. **🏃 Fitness Quests** (Green markers)
   - Complete trails
   - Health-based rewards
   - Temporary stat buffs

4. **📍 Location Quests** (Blue markers)
   - Visit POIs
   - Exploration rewards
   - Discovery bonuses

5. **🤝 Social Quests** (Purple markers)
   - Meet other players
   - Social rewards
   - Friend bonuses

### Quest Rewards Scale With:

- **Rarity**: Common (50 XP) → Legendary (500 XP)
- **Difficulty**: Easy (1x) → Expert (3x)
- **Streaks**: 3-day (+10%), 7-day (+20%), 30-day (+50%)
- **Performance**: High HR = temporary buffs

## Trading System

### How to Trade:

1. Go to Social → Friends
2. Select a friend
3. Tap "Initiate Trade"
4. Add your cards/gold
5. Send offer
6. Wait for counter-offer or acceptance

### Trade Features:

- **Counter-Offers**: Negotiate until both happy
- **Fair Trade Detection**: Warns if >20% value difference
- **Insurance**: Optional 5% fee guarantees execution
- **Reputation**: Track successful trades
- **Templates**: Save favorite trade patterns
- **Escrow**: Cards locked, safe atomic transfer

## PvP Ranked System

### Rank Structure:

```
Bronze IV → Bronze III → Bronze II → Bronze I
Silver IV → Silver III → Silver II → Silver I
Gold IV → Gold III → Gold II → Gold I
Platinum IV → Platinum III → Platinum II → Platinum I
Diamond IV → Diamond III → Diamond II → Diamond I
Master
Grand Master (Top 100)
```

### How Ranking Works:

- **Win**: +20 LP (League Points)
- **Loss**: -15 LP
- **At 100 LP**: Enter promotion series
- **Promotion**: Win 2 of 3 games (division) or 3 of 5 (tier)
- **Streak Bonus**: +3 LP at 3 wins, +5 LP at 5 wins
- **Loss Protection**: Minimal loss at 0 LP

### Gear Normalization:

All gear scaled to same power level in ranked:
- Preserves stat ratios
- Epic still better than Common
- But not drastically overpowered
- Skill > Gear
